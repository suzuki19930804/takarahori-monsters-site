Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the iOS and Android app "宝ほりモンスターズ" (Takarahori Monsters; the "App") handles your information.

Information the App Stores

The App stores player names (the names shown in the game), handicap, quiz and sound settings, trophy counts and similar game data on your device. This data is never sent to the developer's servers.

The "データをコピー" (Copy Data) button in Settings copies your data as text to your device's clipboard. You are responsible for managing the copied data.

Your data is deleted from your device when you delete the App. It may be included in device backups (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), which are handled according to each service's privacy policy.

Advertising

To keep the App free, it shows ads after matches. Ads are delivered through LevelPlay, an ad mediation service by Unity Technologies (including Unity Ads and ironSource Ads).

To deliver ads, measure impressions and taps, and prevent fraud, information such as your device model, OS version, language setting, IP address, and records of ad impressions and taps may be sent to these ad providers. This information is handled according to Unity's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be played by children. Accordingly:

  • The App tells its ad providers that it is directed to children (subject to the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA), and is set up not to show ads based on your interests or behavior (personalized ads).
  • On iOS, the App never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • The App never asks for personal information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. We recommend using a nickname rather than a real name for player names.

Technical Information About the App

The App is built with the Unity game engine. To find and fix problems, the Unity engine may send technical information such as your device model and OS version, and details about crashes, to Unity Technologies. This information does not identify you. It is handled according to Unity's Privacy Policy.

The App does not use any other analytics or tracking services.

Sharing With Third Parties

Except for the ad providers and Unity Technologies described above, we never share your information with third parties.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us through the Support page.

Changes

We may update this policy as needed. When we do, we will post the changes on this page with the date of the last update.
